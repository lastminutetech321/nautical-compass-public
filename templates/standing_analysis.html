Run a Standing Check

Enter basic complaint facts to score your standing under the Lujan three-part test.

1. Injury in Fact

Concrete, particularized, actual or imminent harm — not speculative.

  • Concrete: real, not abstract
  • Particularized: affects the plaintiff personally
  • Actual or imminent: not conjectural

2. Causation

Injury fairly traceable to the challenged conduct, not an independent third party.

3. Redressability

Likely — not merely speculative — that a favorable decision will redress the injury.
