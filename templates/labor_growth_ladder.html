Career DNA

Worker Growth Ladder

Five tiers from entry crew to production lead. Each tier shows the skills, certifications, and experience that unlock the next level of dispatch opportunity.

{# Tier 1 #}
Tier 1 — Entry Crew

Stagehand & General Labor

Score range: 30–45

Physical labor, load-in/load-out, basic rigging assist, cable run. No certifications required to start.

General Labor Stagehand

Unlock Tier 2: Complete OSHA 10 or obtain a CDL, forklift cert, or equivalent trade credential.

{# Tier 2 #}
Tier 2 — Skilled Crew

Certified Stagehand

Score range: 46–60

OSHA-certified crew, forklift operators, CDL drivers, lift-cert holders. Eligible for lead crew on smaller calls.

OSHA 10/30 CDL Forklift Lift Cert

Unlock Tier 3: Develop a specialized AV trade skill — audio, video, or lighting tech.

{# Tier 3 #}
Tier 3 — Specialized Tech

A2 / V2 / L2 / Rigger

Score range: 61–75

Journeyman-level AV technicians, production riggers, and video operators. Can run a system under a department head.

Audio A2 Video V2 Lighting L2 Rigging

Unlock Tier 4: Achieve department-head certification (A1, V1, L1) or obtain ETCP.

{# Tier 4 #}
Tier 4 — Lead Tech

A1 / V1 / L1 / ETCP

Score range: 76–90

Department-head AV technicians and ETCP-certified riggers. Capable of designing and running full systems independently.

Audio A1 Video V1 Lighting L1 ETCP

Unlock Tier 5: Build production coordination and crew management experience.

{# Tier 5 #}
Tier 5 — Production Lead

Stage Manager / Crew Lead / Coordinator

Score range: 91–100

Full production leadership — crew management, show calling, multi-department coordination. Top-tier dispatch priority across all markets.

Stage Manager Crew Lead Production Coordinator

Sustain: Maintain verified credentials, update market area, and keep availability current.
