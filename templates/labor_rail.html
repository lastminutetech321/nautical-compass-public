How readiness is scored

When you submit a labor intake, the system scores your profile 0–100 across five readiness dimensions: role type, availability, location, documentation on file, and union status. A score of 80 or above marks your profile as Crew Ready — eligible for dispatch review. Missing items are listed with specific next steps.
