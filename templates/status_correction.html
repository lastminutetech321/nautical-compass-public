Credit Record Correction

Dispute inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information on your credit report under the FCRA (15 U.S.C. § 1681i).

  • File dispute with the credit bureau directly (TransUnion, Equifax, Experian)
  • File dispute with the data furnisher (lender, creditor)
  • Bureau must investigate within 30 days (45 days if you provide documentation)
  • Unverifiable information must be deleted
FCRA Rights

Worker Misclassification

If you were classified as an independent contractor but should have been an employee, you may have claims for unpaid benefits, taxes, and wages.

  • IRS behavioral/financial/relationship test
  • ABC test (state-dependent) for wage law purposes
  • DOL economic reality test for FLSA coverage
  • Retroactive reclassification may entitle you to overtime, benefits, and employer tax contributions

Public Record Errors

Inaccurate court records, criminal history, or government-held data can be challenged through formal correction procedures.

  • Court orders for record correction or sealing
  • Criminal record expungement (state procedures vary)
  • Background check inaccuracies — FCRA § 1681k procedures
  • FOIA requests to inspect and challenge agency records

Employment Status Correction

Challenge incorrect employment classifications, termination records, and reference information.

  • Defamation claims for false employment references
  • EEOC charge for discriminatory misclassification
  • Unemployment insurance reclassification appeals
  • Demand letters for record correction under applicable state law
